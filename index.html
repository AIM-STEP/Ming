Future plan

Reshaping how medical evidence is evaluated, synthesised and translated in the age of AI, against a rising tide of AI-assisted study output. Three connected fronts:

  1. Transparent, dynamic evidence ecosystems

    Integrate evidence-based medicine with AI across the full evidence lifecycle, so synthesis keeps pace with output without losing traceability.

  2. From prediction to navigation

    Move beyond predictive risk scores towards navigational tools that carry evidence to a specific decision at the bedside.

  3. Proportional evidentiary standards

    Match the strength of a clinical claim to the rigour of the evidence behind it, and make that match explicit.
