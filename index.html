Evidence-based medicine · Digital health

Ming Liu

MD, PhD · Clinician-scientist

Evidence to inform better clinical decisions.

I am a clinician-scientist working at the intersection of evidence-based medicine and digital health. My research focuses on evidence synthesis, the credibility of clinical research, and the use of AI to connect research with bedside decisions.

01 / Research

Questions that guide my work.

01

Clinical evidence synthesis

Systematic reviews and network meta-analyses in infection, immunology, and oncology, including antibiotic duration, antivirals, and autoimmune and allergic diseases.

Systematic reviewsNetwork meta-analysisClinical evidence

02

Evidence methods & credibility

Individual participant data meta-analysis, subgroup credibility, risk of bias, GRADE absolute effects, and transparency in core outcome set development.

GRADEIPD meta-analysisCore outcome sets

03

AI & evidence translation

Developing approaches that integrate AI with evidence appraisal and synthesis, with a focus on transparent evidence systems and decisions at the bedside.

Digital healthAI for evidenceDecision support

Research direction

I aim to connect evidence-based medicine with AI across the evidence lifecycle: building transparent, dynamic evidence systems, linking evidence to clinical decisions, and matching the strength of clinical claims to the rigor of supporting evidence.
